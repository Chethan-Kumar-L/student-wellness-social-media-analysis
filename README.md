# 🧠 Student Wellness & Social Media Impact Study

## 🎯 Background & Objective
Social media platforms offer connectivity, but overuse can severely impact sleep patterns, mental health, and academic productivity among students. Working as part of a mental wellness research team, 
the objective of this project was to uncover demographic trends, usage patterns, and behavioral risk correlations to guide digital detox strategies.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
* **Data Cleaning & Analysis:** Python (`Pandas`, `NumPy`, `Matplotlib`, `Seaborn`)
* **Dashboarding & Visualization:** Power BI (Star Schema data modeling, DAX measures, time-intelligence, bookmarks, and drill-throughs)
* **Feature Engineering:** Created custom risk-level tiers (Low, Medium, High) based on daily usage hours.

## 📊 Key Analytical Steps & EDA
1. **Data Cleaning:** Loaded demographic and platform usage sheets, verified data types, and handled missing values.
2. **Aggregations & Grouping:** Used Pandas groupbys to analyze average addiction levels and sleep deprivation across genders, age brackets, and education levels.
3. **Data Visualization:** Built multi-page Power BI reports featuring executive summaries, lifestyle metrics, and relationship conflict analyses.

## 💡 Strategic Insights & Recommendations
* **The Sleep-Usage Tradeoff:** Students exceeding daily usage thresholds showed a direct negative correlation with nightly sleep hours and academic performance bands.
* **Targeted Interventions:** High addiction scores clustered around specific demographics, indicating that digital detox campaigns should be customized rather than generalized.
* **Actionable Next Steps:** Recommended deploying automated digital wellness tracking and time-management workshops within educational institutions.
